Our Story

From a backyard hive to your favourite local.

It started with a single hive Mr Bee inherited from his grandfather, a trickle of honey, and a wild idea: what if you could taste a meadow in a pint?

A decade later, we still brew everything by hand in small batches in our converted barn. We share it across the bar with the same neighbours who first cheered us on, alongside the new friends who keep finding their way to our door.

  • Independent & family-run
  • Carbon-neutral brewhouse
  • Spent grain donated to local farms
