Our Story

From a single hive to a beloved local institution.

It started with one hive, a grandfather's legacy, and the kind of curiosity that refuses to stay quiet. Mr Bee set out to answer a simple question: could you taste a wildflower meadow in a pint of beer?

Ten years later, the answer is still yes — and we've got forty hives and twenty-seven awards to prove it. Everything is brewed by hand in our converted Victorian barn, using honey harvested that same morning.

  • 🌱
    Carbon-neutral brewhouse Solar-powered, zero-waste processing
  • 🐝
    Estate apiary All honey sourced from our own forty hives
  • 🌾
    Circular brewing Spent grain donated to three local farms
